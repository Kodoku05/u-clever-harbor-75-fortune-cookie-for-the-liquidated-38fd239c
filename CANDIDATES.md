# Fortune candidates

Scores are editorial judgments, not measured judge results. Each criterion is
scored 1–5: C1 = fortune plus trader/loss relevance; C2 = warm humor about the
loss; C3 = one-breath brevity; C4 = freshness; C5 = fortune voice. Criteria are
prioritized in that order, rather than treated as an equally weighted total.

| # | Candidate | C1 | C2 | C3 | C4 | C5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A great fortune awaits you — just not the one in your trading account. | 4 | 4 | 5 | 3 | 5 |
| 2 | The cookie says: your next trade will be legendary. It does not say profitable. | 3 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 3 |
| 3 | Your lucky numbers are 0, 0, 0, and 0. The cookie, unlike your exchange, still believes in you. | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 4 |
| 4 | Your liquidated crypto account foretells a long life: you can no longer afford a midlife crisis. | 5 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 5 |
| 5 | After crypto liquidation, great things await you. Fortunately, the cookie was included with dinner. | 5 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| 6 | Your liquidated crypto account shall reunite you with family. They have a spare room. | 5 | 3 | 5 | 4 | 4 |

1. Strong supplied baseline. The money/fortune double meaning works, but the
   loss is implied and the reversal is fairly familiar.
2. A clean final word, but it predicts a future trade without establishing a
   liquidation. The explanatory cookie framing also weakens the oracle voice.
3. Warm and recognizably a cookie slip. The repeated zeros consume space,
   and the ending is reassurance more than a fresh punchline.
4. Winner. Explicit crypto liquidation anchors the situation. A traditional
   long-life prediction unexpectedly becomes a joke about the price of a
   midlife crisis. It teases the trader's empty account without moralizing.
5. Concrete dinner scene, but the free-cookie consolation is a smaller laugh.
6. Concrete spare room and a late turn, but implying lost housing can feel
   harsh to someone freshly liquidated.

## Final refinement

Draft: Your liquidated crypto account foretells a long life: you can no longer afford a midlife crisis.

Final: Your crypto liquidation foretells longevity: you can no longer afford a midlife crisis.

Compress the account description to the event itself; replace “a long life”
with the more oracular “longevity.” Keep the final “midlife crisis” intact as
the surprise. This original beats the supplied baseline on explicit relevance
and freshness, with a small brevity tradeoff. These are subjective scores.
